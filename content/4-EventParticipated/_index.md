During my internship, I participated in 2 events. Each event was a memorable experience with new, interesting, and useful knowledge, along with gifts and wonderful moments.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Prompt Engineering, AI Mind Mapping & BMAD Method

&emsp;**Date & Time:** May 09, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Description & Value Gained:** The event focused on three main topics: Automated Prompt Engineering to optimize LLM output quality, applying mind maps (the KFC model) for effective AI interaction, and getting familiar with the BMAD development framework. Through the event, I learned how to write precise prompts, understood token mechanisms and advanced techniques like CoT and RAG, while also grasping how to integrate AI on AWS Serverless architecture.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS Vietnam Community Day 2026

&emsp;**Date & Time:** May 23, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Description & Value Gained:** The event gathered experts to share practical experiences in building AI systems and cloud infrastructure on AWS. The topics ranged from context management for AI (Second Brain) and enterprise-grade multi-agent systems, to infrastructure optimization with Amazon CloudFront and real-world lessons from hackathons. The event helped me realize deeply that AI only truly delivers sustainable value when combined with product thinking, robust system architecture, and strict security guardrails on the cloud.
